# Hotel guest experience

The hotel welcomes guests through its guest page and helps them plan their stay
through the concierge.

## Language

**Guest page**:
The hotel's welcome page for exploring rooms, amenities, the neighbourhood, and
booking a stay.
_Avoid_: Product showcase, operator dashboard

**Concierge**:
The guest assistant for questions about reservations, the hotel, and the local area.
_Avoid_: Sales bot
